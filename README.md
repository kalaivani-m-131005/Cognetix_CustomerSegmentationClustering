# Customer Segmentation using Clustering

## Internship Project - Cognetix Technology

This project performs customer segmentation using Machine Learning techniques to group customers based on their shopping behavior, income, and spending patterns.

The project uses K-Means Clustering to identify different customer groups and provide business recommendations for targeted marketing and customer retention.

---

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to:
- Analyze customer shopping behavior
- Segment customers into meaningful groups
- Identify high-value and low-engagement customers
- Support personalized marketing strategies
- Improve customer retention and business decision-making

## Features Used for Clustering

The following customer attributes were used:
- Income
- Recency
- Total Spending

Total Spending was calculated using:
- Wine Purchases
- Fruit Purchases
- Meat Product Purchases
- Fish Product Purchases
- Sweet Product Purchases
- Gold Product Purchases

---

## Machine Learning Algorithm Used

This project uses:
- K-Means Clustering

K-Means clustering groups customers with similar purchasing behaviors into different customer segments.

---

## Customer Segments Identified

- High Spenders
- Loyal Customers
- Discount Seekers
- Low Engagement Users

## Key Insights

- Customer groups showed different spending behaviors.
- High income customers often demonstrated higher spending patterns.
- Some customers preferred discounts and lower spending levels.
- Clustering successfully identified distinct customer groups.
- Customer segmentation supports personalized business strategies.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Provide premium offers and loyalty rewards for high spenders.
- Offer personalized discounts to discount seekers.
- Increase engagement campaigns for low engagement customers.
- Maintain retention programs for loyal customers.
- Use customer segmentation for targeted marketing strategies.
